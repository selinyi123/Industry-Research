# China ITO Targets — Monitoring Run 2026-08-25

## Executive synthesis

No evidence found that warrants revising the current 2025 China ITO physical-demand midpoint (~1.48 kt) or the 2030 base-case demand (~2.03 kt). The material update is upstream price transmission: indium remains at a very high level in August 2026 and industry spot commentary now explicitly describes traditional ITO-target procurement as cautious/conservative. This strengthens the existing thesis that 2026 revenue growth and tonnage growth must be separated.

## Conflict register

### C-20260825-001 — price vs volume
- Observation: indium remains around CNY 5.5k/kg, while traditional ITO procurement is described as conservative.
- Conflict risk: nominal ITO sales revenue can rise from raw-material pass-through even if physical tonnage is flat or down.
- Treatment: continue to model `volume × indium-price component × processing premium × recycling/settlement` separately.

### C-20260825-002 — commercial growth claim vs verified demand model
- Reported: 9.5% ITO demand growth (broad commercial report page).
- Existing base model: China ITO physical demand 2025-2030 CAGR ~6.5%.
- Resolution: no revision until the 9.5% figure is traceable to a defined ITO market boundary and independent primary/industry-association evidence.
